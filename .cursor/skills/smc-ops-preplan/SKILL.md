# Ops Pre-Plan
Before Plan slicing freeze deployment topology impact, compatibility window, environment/config changes,
health/readiness signals, migration/startup order, rollback strategy and required LIVE verification. Topology or
irreversible migration changes force FULL governance. Do not claim a deployment has succeeded; final LIVE
candidate/evidence remains owned by GES Delivery.
