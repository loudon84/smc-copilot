# v1.2 Observation templates

Do not mark Campaign `SUCCEEDED` automatically after batches pass.

## Canary (batch 0)

- Size: 2 endpoints.
- Observe: ≥ 24 hours.
- Record: Action result, checksum, owner, Gateway availability, Work Chat smoke (operator/harness), Compact Diagnostic redaction.

## Follow-on batches

- Size: ≤ 5 endpoints.
- Observe: ≥ 6 hours.
- Advance only when all targets `HEALTHY`, gate `PASS`, and next-batch dual approval is recorded against the current revision.

## Final 7-Day Observation

After every batch has passed, observe the whole snapshot for 7 days. Operator signs `proven` only on the evidence PR. Cursor/API leave `verification=implemented` and `decision=NO-GO`.
